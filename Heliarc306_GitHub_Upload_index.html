    REFERENCE / MACHINE NOTES

    Your 306: terminology & limits

    What the exact front-panel labels mean
    • CURRENT RANGE: LOW 3–50 A / HIGH 20–380 A. Use the matching inner/outer CURRENT CONTROL scale, and verify with the actual AMPERES meter under load.
    • AC / DCSP / DCRP: AC for normal aluminum TIG. DCSP = electrode negative (standard steel/stainless TIG). DCRP = electrode positive (common stick rods).
    • HIGH FREQUENCY: START for DC TIG, CONTINUOUS for AC TIG, OFF for stick.
    • BALANCE: On your machine, the label reads "DC TIG, STICK / AC TIG (MAX CLEAN)" at the cleaning end and "AC TIG ONLY (MAX PENETRATION)" at the other end. Clockwise toward penetration on AC; it's not a modern percentage dial.
    • POST FLOW: 3–45 seconds, keeps hot tungsten shielded after the arc ends.
    • SOFT START / SLOPE TIME UP-DOWN / FINAL CURRENT / SPOT WELD TIME: optional sequence module. Basic setups keep the slope feature OFF and do not use spot timing.
    • ARC FORCE: stick only; generally MIN for TIG.
    • LOW AMP MODULE: DC low range only, labeled max 50 A at 16% duty cycle when engaged. OFF unless intentionally using it and following its limits.
    • REMOTE CONTACTOR / REMOTE CURRENT: verify pedal/switch compatibility and all connections. Set PANEL/REMOTE accordingly.
    How the amperage and gas estimates are calculated

    TIG base current comes from a 24-gauge to 1/2-inch manual reference chart. The app chooses a practical midpoint and applies modest suggested variations for joint type and position. These are not manufacturer-programmed settings or weld qualifications. Argon/helium and argon/hydrogen can make the arc hotter; the app does not claim a universal amperage conversion. It displays starting current plus qualitative adjustments and gas flow ranges.

    The TIG gas validator refuses CO₂, oxygen-bearing MIG gases and nitrogen, because changes to polarity or amperage do not make those gases appropriate for normal TIG. Shielding gas is not needed for coated stick electrodes.
